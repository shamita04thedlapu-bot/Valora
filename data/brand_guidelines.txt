VALORA DEMO BRAND GUIDELINES

BRAND TONE
- Professional
- Clear
- Friendly
- Helpful
- Avoid excessive slang or casual language

PREFERRED TERMINOLOGY
- Use "customer" instead of "user"
- Use "product" instead of "item"
- Use clear and simple language

CLAIMS
- Do not make unsupported claims
- Avoid phrases such as "best in the world", "100% guaranteed", "number one", or "works for everyone"
- Claims involving statistics must be supported by evidence

ACCESSIBILITY
- Use short and clear sentences
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Avoid writing entire sentences in uppercase
- Make content easy to understand

MESSAGING
- Do not make misleading promises
- Clearly communicate what the product actually does
- Avoid exaggerated marketing language
